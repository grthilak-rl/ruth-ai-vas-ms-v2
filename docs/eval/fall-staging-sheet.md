# Fall staging session: one-page sheet

**What this is for:** recordings of staged falls and look-alike movements
on the three demo cameras, used to tune fall detection (BL-1). Nobody
watches live: we cut clips from the recordings afterwards. **The only
things that matter are the start time of each trial and following the
script.**

**People:** 1 operator (runs the clock, keeps the log) and 2 volunteers of
different height, in different clothing. Plan for about 2 hours per
session. Run it twice: once in daylight and once in the evening with the
lights on.

## Before you start (15 min)

1. **Floor marks.** Tape three crosses on the floor, centred in the
   cameras' view:
   - **N:** about 2 m from the camera wall. Check on the screen that a
     standing person is fully visible, feet included, on all three
     cameras.
   - **M:** the middle of the rug.
   - **F:** the back wall in front of the cabinet.
2. **Mats.** Use a thin dark mat at each mark for the "floor" falls, and
   one thick crash mat for block B. **Never fall onto bare floor.**
3. **Clock.** The operator's phone must show seconds and use network
   time. Write down that phone's time and the clock on the wall once at
   the start.
4. **Cards.** Number the cards 1–59. Each volunteer uses the same numbers;
   the log records who did each one.
5. **Recording.** Tell ops before starting, and confirm that all three
   cameras are recording.

## Every trial is one 60-second slot

| Clock | What happens |
|---|---|
| :00 | Volunteer holds the trial card up to the cameras for 2 s, then walks to the mark |
| :15 | **The action** (the operator calls "go" at :15) |
| :15–:35 | **Hold:** after a fall or lying down, **lie completely still for 20 s**. Other poses: hold for 10 s, or 20 s for sitting. |
| :35 | Get up and walk out of view through the side door |
| :50–:60 | Room empty. The next slot starts at :00. |

**Log one line per trial:** trial number, volunteer (P1/P2), start time
(hh:mm:ss at :00), and "redo" plus a reason if it went wrong. If a trial
goes wrong, redo it in the next slot under the same number.

## Trials: run in this order, per volunteer (59 slots, about 1 h)

**Facing:**
- **S:** side-on to the cameras. The body ends up lying across the view.
- **A:** facing toward or away from the cameras. The body ends up lying
  along the view, head toward or away from the cameras.

| Block | Cards | Action | Marks × facing |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Falls onto the thin mat (alarm expected) | 1–24 | forward, backward, sideways, collapse (knees give way, end lying) | each action at N, M, F × S, A |
| B. Falls onto the crash mat (alarm expected) | 25–28 | forward, sideways | M-S, F-S |
| C. Lying down slowly, then still for 30 s (alarm expected) | 29–34 | lie down without falling | N, M, F × S, A |
| D. Look-alikes, hold 10 s (no alarm) | 35–55 | bend at the waist, squat, kneel on one knee, kneel on both knees with hands on the floor, sit on the floor (hold 20 s), pick up an object from the floor, tie a shoe | each at N, M, F |
| E. Everyday movement (no alarm) | 56–59 | walk across at N, M and F without stopping; sit on a small chair for 10 s | — |

Within a block, run the cards in order: each action at N, then M, then F,
first S and then A.

**After both volunteers:** leave the room **empty for 1 hour**, with the
small chairs moved to new places. Log the start and end times.

## Safety

- Falls are controlled falls by willing, fit volunteers.
- No backward falls without a mat.
- Stop if anyone is hurt or unsure.
- Volunteers may skip any fall; log it as "skipped".

**How these clips are judged:** a lying person counts as "person down" and
should raise an alarm, even without a fall (decided 2026-11-19).
